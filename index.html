What changes, and why

  • Slim top bar.The food starts on the first screen.
  • Order box and one main offer.Prices are real text, with a single "Order now" button.
  • Swipeable, sticky categories.Customers always know where they are in the menu.
  • Cart bar at the bottom.It appears only once something is in the cart.
  • Branch-aware menu, Arabic and English.Only what is available at the chosen branch is shown.

Two ways to get there

Option A: Fix the current site

2 to 4 weeks. Low cost, low risk. Start here.

  • Keep WordPress and WooCommerce
  • Remove extra page builders and duplicate add-ons
  • Fix caching so pages load in under a second
  • Rebuild home, menu and cart to match the prototype

Option B: Rebuild the front end

3 to 4 months. Higher cost, app-like feel.

  • New, very fast front end served close to customers
  • WooCommerce or a restaurant system linked to the tills
  • Arabic, Apple Pay, Google Pay, rewards, order tracking
  • Makes a future mobile app easier
